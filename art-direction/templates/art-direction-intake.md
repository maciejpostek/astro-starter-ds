# Art Direction Intake

Status: template.

Use this checklist before drafting a project Brand Expression Contract.

## 1. Brand Inputs

- [ ] Brand strategy or positioning is supplied.
- [ ] Audience and context of use are known.
- [ ] Existing identity guidelines are supplied.
- [ ] Production typefaces and licenses are known.
- [ ] Production color assets are known.
- [ ] Logo and brand-mark assets are supplied.
- [ ] Imagery or illustration direction is supplied.
- [ ] Accessibility or regulatory constraints are known.

## 2. Visual Evidence

- [ ] At least three relevant references are collected.
- [ ] Every reference records the trait to adopt.
- [ ] Every reference records the trait to avoid or ignore.
- [ ] Reference rights and provenance are recorded.
- [ ] At least one counterexample is included.
- [ ] Existing brand-designer work is preferred over generic inspiration.
- [ ] Figma node IDs or local image paths are stable.

## 3. Creative Interpretation

- [ ] A one-sentence creative thesis is drafted.
- [ ] Visual metaphors are translated into observable relationships.
- [ ] Hierarchy, contrast, balance, rhythm, and unity are addressed.
- [ ] Composition and grid behavior are defined.
- [ ] Typography behavior is defined.
- [ ] Color allocation and surface logic are defined.
- [ ] Spacing, shape, border, and elevation behavior are defined.
- [ ] Imagery, iconography, and motion behavior are defined when relevant.
- [ ] Non-negotiables and degrees of freedom are separated.

## 4. Calibration

- [ ] Button is represented.
- [ ] Input or FormField is represented.
- [ ] Tag is represented.
- [ ] ContentBlock or SectionHeader is represented.
- [ ] One representative Card is represented.
- [ ] Navigation is represented.
- [ ] One representative Hero or section is represented.
- [ ] The calibration set is reviewed together on one page.
- [ ] Desktop, intermediate, and mobile behavior are reviewed.
- [ ] Long and short content are reviewed.

## 5. Approval

- [ ] The contract has a named owner.
- [ ] References are individually approved or rejected.
- [ ] Component signatures are approved.
- [ ] Visual QA criteria are agreed.
- [ ] The contract status is changed to `approved`.
- [ ] Approval date and reviewer are recorded.

Until all mandatory project inputs exist, keep the project contract in
`not-configured`, `draft`, or `review`.
