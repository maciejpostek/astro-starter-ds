## 6. Hierarchy, Contrast, Balance, Rhythm, And Unity

Explain how the project creates:

- hierarchy;
- meaningful contrast;
- local and global balance;
- repetition and rhythm;
- cross-component unity;
- controlled tension.

## 7. Composition And Grid

Define:

- container behavior;
- column and baseline logic;
- dominant alignments;
- common span families;
- symmetry or asymmetry;
- vertical rhythm;
- negative-space rules;
- responsive transformations.

## 8. Typography

Define:

- licensed typefaces;
- display and functional roles;
- semantic role relationships;
- measure;
- weight, line-height, and tracking behavior;
- casing;
- responsive behavior;
- optical exceptions.

## 9. Color

Define:

- primitive source;
- semantic roles;
- dominant and supporting surfaces;
- accent allocation;
- state colors;
- contrast expectations;
- Light and Dark behavior;
- forbidden combinations.

## 10. Spacing, Shape, And Surface

Define:

- density;
- internal and external spacing relationships;
- radius and edge treatment;
- borders;
- elevation;
- recurring signature details;
- optical corrections.

## 11. UI Component DNA

Link the approved component signatures. Explain the shared traits that must
appear across different component roles.

## 12. Imagery, Illustration, Iconography, And Motion

Define only the categories relevant to the project. Record source, rights,
cropping, geometry, pacing, and Reduced Motion behavior.

## 13. Responsive Expression

Describe how the visual character transforms rather than merely shrinks.

## 14. Non-Negotiables

List decisions every generated design must preserve.

## 15. Degrees Of Freedom

List areas where humans and AI may explore without changing the brand contract.

## 16. Approved References

Reference `reference-manifest.json`. Do not paste untraceable inspiration.

## 17. Calibration Set

Link approved Figma nodes or browser routes for Button, Input or FormField, Tag,
ContentBlock or SectionHeader, Card, Navigation, and one Hero or section.

## 18. Validation And Approval

Record:

- contract owner;
- reviewers;
- approval state;
- approval date;
- replaced contract;
- known gaps.
