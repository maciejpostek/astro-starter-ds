# Product brief — handoff from day one

Replace this template with the group's reviewed brief and remove the template
marker. Preserve shared brand strategy; this file describes the specific product
and campaign. Retain original source links and distinguish facts, approved
hypotheses and unknowns. An AI-generated product is not evidence of market results.

## Product and capabilities
Name, purpose, current capabilities, prototype link or screenshots.

## Audience / ICP
Who is this campaign for? What problem motivates them?

## Value proposition
What useful outcome does the product offer? Which differentiators are supported?

## Page goal and CTA
One primary goal and call to action; destination or missing destination.

## Language and tone
Campaign language and any approved refinements of the brand voice.

## Evidence and assumptions
Day-one decisions, source references, approved hypotheses and unknowns.

## Content and visuals
Available product content, asset references and gaps. Missing prices or visuals
must not prevent drafting independent sections. Do not fabricate missing assets.
